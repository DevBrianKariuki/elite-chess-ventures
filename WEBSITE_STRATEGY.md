### FAQ Section
**Purpose:** Address objections preemptively

**Questions:**

**For Schools:**
1. "How does chess fit into our curriculum?"
   Answer: Our programs complement STEM education and co-curricular requirements. Chess can be integrated during games period, club activities, or as an elective.

2. "What equipment do we need to provide?"
   Answer: Nothing. We provide all chess sets, boards, clocks, and teaching materials. We also handle classroom setup.

3. "How do you measure student progress?"
   Answer: We provide termly reports showing skill progression, tournament participation, and behavioral improvements. Schools can request parent-teacher meeting materials.

4. "What are your coach qualifications?"
   Answer: All our coaches are FIDE-certified, background-checked, and trained in child safeguarding. Most have teaching experience and competitive chess backgrounds.

**For Parents:**
5. "My child has never played chess. Can they start?"
   Answer: Absolutely! We specialize in teaching beginners. Our coaches make learning fun and age-appropriate, starting with basic concepts.

6. "How long until my child sees results?"
   Answer: Most students show improved focus and problem-solving within 8-12 weeks. Competitive readiness typically takes 6-12 months depending on commitment.

7. "Do you offer online coaching?"
   Answer: Yes! We offer both in-person and online sessions via Zoom. Online sessions include interactive chess boards and recorded lessons.

8. "What if my child doesn't enjoy it?"
   Answer: We offer a free trial session so there's no risk. If they don't enjoy it after the trial, there's no obligation to continue.

## 5. Content Copywriting Principles

### Brand Voice:
- **Tone:** Professional yet warm and approachable
- **Style:** Clear, benefit-focused, conversational
- **Personality:** Trustworthy expert, passionate educator, results-driven

### Copywriting Guidelines:

**Headlines:**
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Use power words: "Transform," "Build," "Develop," "Master"
- Keep under 10 words
- Address the audience directly (Your child, Your students)

**Subheadlines:**
- Clarify and expand the headline
- Include specific benefits or proof
- 15-25 words optimal

**Body Copy:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points for scannability
- Active voice
- Benefits before features
- Address pain points
- Use Kenyan context and examples

**CTAs:**
- Action-oriented verbs
- Specific and clear
- Create urgency or remove friction
- Examples: "Book Free Demo Today," "Start Your Free Trial"

### Emotional Triggers:
1. **For Parents:**
   - Pride in child's achievement
   - Fear of missing out on development opportunities
   - Desire for academic improvement
   - Social status (child's accomplishments)

2. **For Schools:**
   - Differentiation from competitors
   - Student engagement and retention
   - Academic reputation enhancement
   - Ease of implementation

3. **For Corporates:**
   - Brand visibility
   - Community impact
   - Employee engagement
   - CSR objectives
